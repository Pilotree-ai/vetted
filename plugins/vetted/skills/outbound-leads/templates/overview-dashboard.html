Sequences

Sequences appear here once Apollo data has loaded.

Queued is the emails Apollo has scheduled now: one per active contact, their next step. Delivered, Bounced and Replied count emails. People emailed is how many got the first email. Reply rate is replies divided by people emailed. Slots left is the mailbox's daily limit from Apollo, minus what it has sent today and what the live sequences will send.

A campaign groups the batches of one offer by sequence name ("UK agencies - US batch 2" belongs to "UK agencies"). Daily activity comes from Apollo's analytics, one record per day.

The forecast is an estimate. It assumes follow-ups go out on their step day (weekdays only), each sequence stays within its own cap, and the mailbox never sends more than its daily limit. Anything over the limit waits for the next day. Refreshed from Apollo every morning by the outbound-leads skill. Anyone who can edit this page can also refresh it with Refresh from Apollo, which only reads from Apollo, using their own Apollo connection.
